Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 16, 2026

TripAStudios ("TripAStudios," "we," "us," or "our") develops and publishes Partbound (the "Game").

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Partbound. It describes the information the Game currently collects, stores, or processes, as well as how our practices may change as additional features are introduced.

1. Information We Collect

Current Version

The current version of Partbound does not require you to create an account and does not intentionally collect personal information such as your name, email address, physical address, or phone number.

The Game may store gameplay information locally on your device, such as game progress and player build information, in order to provide the Game's functionality.

Information that remains solely on your device is not transmitted to TripAStudios unless the Game is specifically designed to transmit that information to us or to a third-party service.

Future Online Features

Future versions of Partbound may introduce online features, including player accounts, online game-state synchronization, player builds, leaderboards, and other server-based functionality.

If these features are introduced, this Privacy Policy will be updated to describe the information collected or transmitted, the purposes for which it is used, how long it is retained, and the circumstances under which it may be shared with third parties.

2. How We Use Information

Information handled by Partbound may be used for purposes such as:

  • Saving and restoring gameplay progress.
  • Providing and maintaining Game functionality.
  • Storing and retrieving player builds when online functionality is introduced.
  • Operating and maintaining online Game services when available.
  • Preventing cheating, abuse, fraud, or manipulation of Game systems when online functionality is introduced.
  • Maintaining leaderboards and other online features when those features become available.

The current version of Partbound does not use personal information for advertising or marketing purposes.

3. Advertising

The current version of Partbound does not display advertisements.

Future versions of Partbound may include optional advertisements provided by third-party advertising services. If advertising is introduced, this Privacy Policy will be updated to identify the relevant advertising providers and describe the types of information they may collect or process and the purposes for which that information is used.

4. In-App Purchases

The current version of Partbound does not offer in-app purchases.

Future versions of Partbound may offer optional purchases through Google Play or other applicable payment services. If in-app purchases are introduced, this Privacy Policy will be updated as necessary to describe the relevant data handling and third-party services involved.

5. Third-Party Services

The current version of Partbound does not intentionally transmit personal information to third-party services.

Future versions of the Game may use third-party services to provide functionality such as advertising, authentication, analytics, online game services, payment processing, or other features.

When third-party services are introduced, this Privacy Policy will be updated to identify the relevant services and describe the types of information they may collect or process, where applicable.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by Partbound against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee the absolute security of information.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

The current version of Partbound does not maintain user accounts or an online personal profile.

Gameplay information stored locally on your device may remain there until it is deleted by the Game, cleared through your device's application settings, or removed when the Game is uninstalled.

If future versions of Partbound introduce accounts or server-side user data, we will provide an appropriate mechanism for users to request deletion of applicable accounts and associated personal data. This Privacy Policy will also be updated to explain the applicable data retention and deletion practices.

8. Children's Privacy

Partbound is not specifically directed toward children.

The current version of the Game does not intentionally collect personal information from children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to TripAStudios, please contact us so that we can investigate and take appropriate action.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Partbound's features, data practices, third-party services, or applicable legal or platform requirements change.

When we make changes, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page.

We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically to stay informed about how information is handled by Partbound.
